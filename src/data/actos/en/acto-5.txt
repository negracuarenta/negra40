Text 1

After the meeting with the bird, the tree decided to put an end to its cycle on this earth.

It realised that it would not be able to give the love and the strength that life needs, after hearing the stories its winged friend brought back from its journeys.

But make no mistake, it did not give up.

Before the end it asked the bird for one last favour, now outside their pact.

It said that with its last strength it would go out slowly, so that its wood might become a treasure: the matter of an instrument.

And it asked that, if in flying through the world the bird met a craftsman, it should tell him of its wood, which would be ready to be turned into a musical instrument.

—Let them cut down what is left of me and turn it into sound, since I could give them nothing else.

The old wood at last.

Nothing more.

We still have music, but who knows for how long.

The old wood at last, resounding.

Will there be a song that changes everything?

May someone play, one day, something so beautiful that it evens out so much destruction, so much harm done to oneself.

So much harm done to oneself.
So much harm done to oneself.
So much harm done to oneself.
So much harm done to oneself.
