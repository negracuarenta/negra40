Text 1

An endless red river they call the Nile.

I saw a huge water flower that opens at night and smells of peaches.

I saw a desert that fills with lagoons after the rains, as if the rain were a good mistake in the service of life.

I saw water hollow caves out of stone, and a waterfall that drops three hundred and eighty metres without touching anything.

I saw snow above the equator: Kilimanjaro, white over the savannah.

I saw turtles, dolphins and seabirds, in an archipelago so beautiful that from above it looks painted in watercolour.

And I saw a jaguar, a hypnotic cat, come back after a hundred years to the grassland where its ancestors were raised.

The Guaraní told me there is a land without suffering; they call it "the land without evil". I did not find it. But after so much beauty, I can understand why they look for it.

Tree, the east is thrilling.

Text 2

But I also saw hundreds of rivers that no longer reach the sea, wiped out by droughts and needless diversions.

I saw how the "vaquita marina, the smallest porpoise in the world" was reduced to seven brave survivors.

I saw how the Yangtze paddlefish, which swam two hundred million years before us, did not live to see the year two thousand and three.

The Aral Sea was the fourth largest lake in the world; the maps still draw it blue, but where the water was there have been, for years now, ships stranded in the salt.

And I also saw how Kilimanjaro lost eighty-five per cent of its ice; its glaciers will not see the year two thousand and fifty.

In the Himalaya, which they call the third pole, the ice melts today ten times faster than a century ago. One in every four people on the planet drinks from that ice, and hundreds of ecosystems.

I saw a future without water and it frightened me.

Of the northern white rhinoceros two females remain, guarded day and night by armed men: an entire species reduced to two bodies that can no longer breed.

I saw how in seventy-five years half the insects in the world ceased to exist.

All that, tree, I also saw.
