Victoria Experimental – Concerts of New Music

Date
24 August 2013

Victoria Experimental Concerts of new music

Saturday, August 24, 2013, 8:00 pm Centro Cultural Victoria Ocampo Villa Victoria, Matheu 1851 Mar del Plata, Argentina

Composers Adriana Sasali Martín Virgili Mariano Losi

Guest artists Miguel Canatakis Jorge Ihlenfeld Mariana Pellejero Martín De Lassaletta

Program

Adriana Sasali “No. 3” (2013) Music for strings

Martín Virgili “Tanizaki” (2013) For tape, objects, matches, voices, and strings

Mariano Losi “El agua de Huaco” (2011) For prepared piano and soundtrack

About the concert

The concert brings together the work of two contemporary music composers and a visual artist, each oriented, in their own way, toward an exploration of sound, time, and musical space. These are experimental musics in the double sense of the word: on the one hand, because they themselves are unaware of the aesthetic possibilities of their own unfolding, and on the other, because they somehow lead the audience to revisit its (mythical and historical) assumptions about the experience of sound.
