Rodolfo Acosta – Composing in Residence

Date
12 December 2014

Negra40 presents

Rodolfo Acosta

The Colombian composer Rodolfo Acosta R. is spending a month-long artistic residency in Buenos Aires; the result is “Ludus Australis,” an experimental piece written for the E.N40 ensemble.

Ludus Australis

Performers Sebastián Bauer Mariano Losi Gastón Mazières Martín Virgili

Friday, December 12, 2014, 9:00 pm Sala negra40, Buenos Aires (Palermo)

Tuesday, December 16, 2014, 9:00 pm Negra40, Mar del Plata Club del Teatro, Rivadavia 3422

Tickets: $50.-
