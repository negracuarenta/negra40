Daniel Leguizamón in Concert

Date
22 June 2023

We are very happy to welcome Colombian composer Daniel Leguizamón.

We have prepared two concerts so we can keep discovering his projects, and as a good opportunity to get closer to a refined researcher of Latin American experimentalism.

Concert 1 – En los tiempos: talk-concert

Thursday 6/22 – 7:00 pm Room 27, Conservatorio Manuel de Falla Gallo 238 – CABA

Featuring students from the Contemporary Music Performance program, from the classes of Malena Levin and Inés Sabatini, and from the Advanced Diploma in Contemporary Music.

(…)

Concert 2 – En los tiempos

Friday 6/23, 8:00 pm (sharp) Estudio Desvío Temporal Dr. Guillermo Rawson 930, Morón

An experimental concert together with Daniel Leguizamón (Colombia). World premiere of “Ventana con flores” (“Window with Flowers”) And a set by UDLI (La utilidad de lo inútil / The Usefulness of the Useless).

*In collaboration with Ocho Ojos Producciones

Featuring:

  • Andrea Escobar
  • Ricardo Cuadros Pradilla
  • Maxi Mas
  • Marcelo Von Schultz
  • Jimena Martín
  • Ensamble UDLI (Juan Litrica – Manuel Ogara)
  • Luis Conde
  • Lautaro Abrego
  • Mariano Llere

Many thanks to @luciadrochi for the general coordination of the project.
